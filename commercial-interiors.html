Why Zest for Interiors

An interior firm that builds like an exhibition company.

Exhibitions taught us to deliver finished, branded spaces against dates that never move. We bring that same discipline — and our own fabrication workshop — to permanent showrooms.

Locked to your opening date

We plan backwards from your launch, the same way we plan an exhibition build window. The programme is fixed at BOQ stage and tracked every day on site.

Fixtures made in our workshop

Wall bays, display units, counters and feature walls are fabricated in our Noida workshop while civil work runs on site. Two tracks in parallel — factory finish, shorter site time.

True turnkey, not design-only

Masonry, electrical, plumbing and washrooms are in our scope — not left for you to source. No chasing six contractors; one project manager answers for everything.

Designed to sell, not just to look good

Our layouts start from merchandising: sightlines from the mall corridor, lighting on product, customer flow, trial rooms and the cash counter where they work hardest.

Mall & landlord ready

Fit-out drawings, fire and electrical requirements and site rules handled with the landlord’s team, so approvals don’t hold up your handover.

Itemised, fixed BOQ

Every item, quantity and finish is priced line by line before work starts. The price we agree is the price you pay — no surprise bills at handover.

Questions

Commercial interiors — frequently asked.

Do you really do civil, electrical and plumbing work, or only the interiors?

Yes — that is what turnkey means for us. Masonry, electrical, plumbing and washrooms, ceilings and flooring are all part of our scope, alongside design, fixtures, façade and signage. You deal with one contract and one project manager.

Can you build to our architect’s or brand’s design?

Yes. We work both ways: full design-and-build, or execution to your brand guidelines and your architect’s drawings. Either way the build, finish and handover are our responsibility.

Do you work in malls?

Yes. We prepare fit-out drawings for the landlord, work within mall site rules and timings, and coordinate fire and electrical requirements with the mall’s team.

How long does a showroom take?

It depends on size, site condition and finishes. We give you a fixed programme along with the BOQ, planned backwards from your opening date, and because fixtures are made in our workshop while site work runs, the time on site is shorter.

Can you roll out the same design across several stores?

Yes. Making fixtures in our own workshop lets us standardise them, so every store in a roll-out gets the same finish and the same look.

Where do you work?

We are based in Noida with our own workshop, and we take on commercial interior projects across India.
