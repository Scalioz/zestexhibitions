What We Do

Eleven disciplines. One accountable team.

From the first design sketch to the last screw removed after the show closes — every service below is delivered in-house from our Noida workshop.

Exhibition Stall Designing

Concept to 3D visual, engineered around your brand, floorplan and stall dimensions. Every exhibition stall design is planned for sightlines and footfall before a single material is chosen.

Exhibition Stall Fabrication

In-house workshop build in Noida, quality-controlled from frame to finish. No outsourced vendor chain — design and exhibition stall fabrication under one accountable roof.

Custom Exhibition Booths

Fully bespoke exhibition booth structures for brands that don't want a template. Custom geometry, unusual materials and product-led layouts, engineered for structural safety.

Modular Stall Solutions

Reusable modular exhibition stall systems that pay for themselves across multiple shows. Lower fabrication cost per event after the first build, faster turnaround every time after.

Double Deck Stalls

Two-storey double-deck exhibition stalls engineered for footfall-heavy exhibitions. Doubles usable area on a fixed floor allocation, with structural safety sign-off included.

Country Pavilion Design

Turnkey Exhibition Solutions
