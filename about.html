Our Story

Built by people who'd rather over-engineer than under-deliver.

Zest Exhibitions started in Noida with one belief: an exhibition stall is a three-day-long first impression, and it deserves the same rigour as permanent architecture.

Who We Are

An exhibition stall design and fabrication company built for accountability.

Zest Exhibitions is a Noida-based exhibition stall design and fabrication company serving manufacturing brands, MNCs, government bodies, event agencies, startups and international clients across India and abroad. We were founded on a simple frustration common across the exhibition industry: design studios and fabrication workshops are usually two different vendors, communicating through email chains and site visits, with no single party accountable when a stall doesn't match its render. We closed that gap by building both under one roof — the same team that draws the concept also cuts the material, fabricates the frame, and installs it on the show floor. That single change is why our clients keep coming back for their next exhibition, and their next commercial interior project after that.

Today, that means 250+ exhibition stalls delivered across 18+ industries, from FMCG brand experience booths to government pavilions at international trade fairs, all fabricated in-house and installed on schedule.

Vision

To be the exhibition partner Indian and international brands trust without a second bid.

We want every client's first call for their next show to be the only call they need to make — no competitive re-tender, no second-guessing the finish quality, because the last stall we built for them already answered that question.

Mission

Design and fabricate every stall with the precision of a permanent structure.

An exhibition stall might stand for three days, but the engineering, material choices and finish discipline behind it should be no different from a structure built to stand for thirty years. On schedule, on budget, every single show — that standard doesn't flex based on how long the stall will be up.

FAQs

Common questions about exhibition stalls.

How much does an exhibition stall cost in India?

Cost depends on stall area, open sides, material choice and design complexity. A modular stall typically costs less per square metre than a fully custom build. Share your stall area and requirement on our enquiry form and we'll come back with a detailed quote within 48 hours.

How long does exhibition stall fabrication take?

Most stalls are fabricated in 2–4 weeks once the design is signed off, with installation completed inside the organiser's build window — typically 1–3 days before the show opens. Double-deck and country pavilion builds need more lead time.

Do you handle stall design and fabrication, or just one?

Both, under one roof. Our in-house team in Noida handles design, structural engineering, fabrication, printing, installation and dismantling — so there's a single point of accountability from concept to teardown.

Can you deliver an exhibition stall outside India?

Yes. We've fabricated and shipped stalls for international shows including Dubai and Saudi Arabia, handling customs documentation, freight and on-ground installation for overseas venues.

What's the difference between a modular and a custom exhibition stall?

A modular stall uses a reusable frame system that can be reconfigured for different shows, keeping fabrication cost down over multiple events. A custom stall is a one-off structure built specifically for a single show or brand concept, with no reuse constraints on design.

Do you also do commercial interiors, not just exhibition stalls?

Yes — retail, office and showroom interior design and fit-out, delivered by the same design and fabrication team that builds our exhibition stalls, so the finish quality is consistent across both.

How do I get a quote for my stall?

Fill in your stall area, dimensions, number of open sides and exhibition details on our enquiry form, or message us directly on WhatsApp. We typically respond with a concept direction and quote within 48 hours.
